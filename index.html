How Mariah Responds
She knows the difference between a slip and a spiral.
Four levels of intervention, calibrated to context. You set the tone — gentle, balanced, or strict.
Level 01

The Nudge

You're on track but trending up. Mariah notices and mentions it. No alarms. Just awareness.

"Coffee runs hit $84 this week — that's 30% more than usual. Just keeping you in the loop."
Level 02

The Check-In

You're approaching a budget limit. Mariah asks what you want to do — adjust the budget or rein it back.

"You're at 87% of your monthly dining budget with 9 days left. Want to extend the budget or coast it out?"
Level 03

The Course-Correct

You're over a goal commitment. Mariah names it directly and proposes a fix — and means it.

"You said 4 DoorDash orders/month. You're at 8. Let's fix this — I can block the next one or you can buy yourself out of it."
Level 04

The Intervention

Patterns suggest a real spiral. Mariah pauses auto-investments, locks discretionary spend, and walks you through reset options. You stay in control — she just makes it harder to make the easy mistake.

"This is your third over-budget month. Auto-invest paused. Let's rebuild the plan together — I'll show you three paths back."
